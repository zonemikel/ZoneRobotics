# What is this ?  

These are the "printable" parts for the ZRCar, see the full parts list for the lego parts. You could also print the lego parts if you want but I have not made them yet. You could also copy this from onshape where I made it BUT I warn you my files on onshape are not well organized yet, continue at your own risk 
[onshape heap of files](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/c6df55f4823814be8856a789/w/86bda6ac477b96f6022302d7/e/c94a8f284c20855b457c0eed?renderMode=0&uiState=6ada597ff6baf55bd1fa7ac3)  
I do plan on sharing my features to create lego holes for studs and making videos and explanations about how I made the servos and gears, look out for that pls
